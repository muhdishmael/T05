Australian Television Energy Consumption & Wholesale Electricity Prices

This dashboard presents a comparative visual analysis of appliance energy efficiency across modern television technologies (LCD, LED, and OLED), alongside historical Australian National Electricity Market (NEM) spot power prices from 1998 to 2024.

📺 Models Analyzed: 588 Televisions
🏷️ Tech Covered: LCD, LED, OLED
📅 Electricity Spot Price History: 1998 – 2024
💡 Interactive Features: Tooltips · Legend Filtering · Responsive Scaling
